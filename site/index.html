The problem

Running a clinic shouldn't feel like firefighting.

Carebridge is an operations platform for multi-specialty clinics and small hospitals. Scheduling, patient records, billing and insurance claims, pharmacy stock and staff live in one place, with a live dashboard for the people who run the clinic.

Who we're designing for

AD Clinic owner / administrator
Goal Keep the clinic profitable and running smoothly across departments.
Biggest frustration Learns about problems at month-end, from spreadsheets assembled by hand.
DR Doctor
Goal Spend consult time on the patient, not on paperwork.
Biggest frustration Patient history is scattered across paper files, lab portals and WhatsApp.
FD Front desk
Goal Move patients through quickly and keep the waiting room calm.
Biggest frustration Juggles phone bookings, walk-ins and a register that never matches the doctors’ day.
BI Billing & insurance desk
Goal Get paid in full and on time.
Biggest frustration Claims bounce back for missing documents, and nobody tracks the deadlines.

Pain points and what Carebridge does about them

# Pain point Felt by What Carebridge does Where it lives
01
Unpredictable queues and no-shows
Double bookings, unannounced walk-ins and late-running doctors make waiting times impossible to predict.
 Front desk, patients Live token queue per doctor, automated SMS and WhatsApp reminders, and an overbooking guard on the calendar. Dashboard › Live queue
02
Patient history is scattered
Doctors re-ask the same questions because past visits, prescriptions and reports live in different places.
 Doctors One patient timeline with visits, prescriptions, lab results and uploaded documents. Patients
03
Claim rejections leak revenue
Missing documents and missed deadlines turn billable care into write-offs.
 Billing desk, owner Pre-submission document checklist, deadline alerts and rejection-reason tracking. Dashboard › Alerts, Analytics › Claims
04
No live view of operations
Owners can’t see appointments, waits, beds or revenue until someone compiles a report.
 Owner, administrator A today view with live KPIs for appointments, wait time, bed occupancy, revenue and pending claims. Dashboard
05
Stock-outs and expired medicines
Pharmacy stock is counted by hand, so shortages surface only when a patient needs the drug.
 Pharmacy, administrator Reorder-level and expiry alerts driven by actual consumption, with one-tap purchase orders. Dashboard › Alerts, Inventory
06
Decisions made on gut feel
Staffing, slot planning and department investment happen without data on demand or utilisation.
 Owner, administrator Trends by department, doctor utilisation and no-show patterns by day and time slot. Analytics

Design principles

Glanceable first The most urgent number sits at the top of every screen. An owner should know how today is going in five seconds.
Act where you see it Every alert carries the action that resolves it: notify patients, reorder stock, open the claim. No hunting through menus.
Built for the front desk too Large touch targets, keyboard-friendly booking and readable type for a shared desktop in a busy reception.
